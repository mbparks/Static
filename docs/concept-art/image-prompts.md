# STATIC — House image prompts

Prompts for AI image generators (Midjourney, DALL-E, Flux, etc.). Each House
gets one two-figure prompt; for solo portraits, cut the figure you don't want
and keep everything else. Prepend the **style block** to every prompt — it
carries the world so the House prompts can carry the people.

## The style block (prepend to everything)

> Grimdark post-collapse world, sixty years after a networked civilization
> deliberately destroyed its own infrastructure. Muted, desaturated palette;
> overcast light; textures of patched wool, waxed canvas, worn leather,
> hand-machined metal. Technology is analog, mechanical, deliberately
> disconnected — dials, cranks, stamped steel, paper records. NO neon, NO
> holograms, NO glowing city skylines, NO sleek corporate futurism. The mood
> is quiet dread and stubborn craft: people who survived by refusing to let
> machines listen. Painterly concept art, full-body character study, two
> figures standing on a plain ground line, neutral dark background, character
> sheet composition.

## The Fabricators
*cold iron: machines that cannot listen*

> Two certifiers of the Fabricators: a woman with her hair in a tight bun
> wearing a heavy leather work apron over an iron-grey coat, an amber
> enameled certification seal on her chest, her right forearm sheathed in a
> machined steel stamping gauntlet; and a broad, bearded man in the same
> grey coat and apron carrying a long-hafted certification sledge with an
> amber-inlaid stamp head. Their gear is entirely mechanical — not one
> wire, not one antenna. Tool belts, calipers, punch sets. They look like
> the union that certifies the only machines anyone trusts, because they
> are. Palette: gunmetal grey, soot, one accent of safety amber.

## Recollection
*they consecrate what they may never read*

> Two members of Recollection, the archive order: a hooded woman in layered
> sage-green robes with a cream sash, a small sealed reliquary hanging at
> her chest containing a dark glass shard she is forbidden to read; and a
> tonsured or short-haired man in the same robes holding a heavy paper
> ledger protectively against his body, a cream scroll tube slung at his
> hip. Monastic and bureaucratic at once — ink-stained fingers, wax seals,
> reading lenses on a cord. Their faces are calm and deliberately
> unreadable, people who know more about the end of the old world than
> anyone and say none of it. Palette: sage, grey-green, parchment cream,
> one glint of pale jade.

## The Chirurgeons
*every install is logged; nobody knows what the logs know*

> Two Chirurgeons, the body trade: a woman in a bone-white surgical coat
> with a single dried-blood-red yoke stripe across the shoulders, her right
> arm fully replaced with segmented matte chrome prosthetics — clearly her
> own work; and a stocky man in the same white coat carrying a gleaming
> bone saw, a leather roll of surgical instruments at his belt. Clinical
> but worn: stained cuffs, ledger books of every implant they've ever
> installed chained to their cases. The unsettling warmth of doctors who
> are also a corporation. Palette: bone white, parchment, dried red, dull
> chrome.

## Christianity Inc.
*the quarantine line is a parish boundary*

> Two officers of Christianity Inc., the incorporated church: a woman in a
> severe charcoal corporate suit with a white clerical collar tab, holding
> a small gold censer-lantern on a chain, its coal glowing faintly; and an
> older bearded man in the same corporate-clerical dress carrying a black
> ledger-missal with a gold cross embossed on the cover. Half boardroom,
> half liturgy: lapel pins shaped like a cross inside a rounded trademark
> badge, laminated quarantine-line credentials, rosary wound around a
> clipboard. Faces of people mid-schism — certainty and doubt in the same
> expression. Palette: charcoal, ash black, white collar linen, one
> restrained gold accent.

## The Drovers
*nothing carried speaks*

> Two Drovers of the road: a woman in a dust-brown waxed duster and heavy
> scarf, a sealed teal courier case strapped across her back — banded,
> riveted, openable only by its addressee; and a weathered man in a
> wide-brimmed hat and longer duster, leaning on a tall road staff, a
> second sealed teal case at his hip. Everything about them says distance:
> cracked boots, sun-lines, water skins, hand-drawn route maps. They carry
> the world's data on foot because nothing they carry is allowed to
> transmit. Palette: road dust, umber, saddle leather, one deep teal
> accent on the cases.

## The Lightwrights' Guild
*light, at the price of listening*

> Two line-walkers of the Lightwrights' Guild, the last House to keep the
> old title: a woman in a segmented, quilted grid-navy insulated coat with
> an amber diamond guild patch, carrying a tall line pole topped with a
> warm-glowing lamp; and a bearded man in the same quilted coat with a
> hand-cranked power pack on his hip and a headlamp casting a faint halo.
> Their gear is the compromise they live: light and power, hunted
> constantly for stray signal — signal meters, clip leads, wire shears,
> insulated gauntlets. The warm lamp glow is the only warm light in the
> image. Palette: deep grid navy, slate, warm lamp amber.

## Usage notes

- Aspect: 3:2 or 16:9 for two-figure plates; 2:3 for solo portraits.
- For consistency across a set, keep the style block verbatim and vary only
  the House paragraph.
- Useful negative prompt where supported: `neon, hologram, glowing city,
  cyberpunk street, LED trim, futuristic armor, clean sci-fi, logos, text`.
- The reference field plates these correspond to live in this folder as
  SVGs — same palettes, same props, if the generator supports image
  prompting.
